This page covers all the important events in the sport of **[tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis "Tennis")** in [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023 "2023"). It provides the results of notable tournaments throughout the year on both the [ATP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals "Association of Tennis Professionals") and [WTA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women%27s_Tennis_Association "Women's Tennis Association") Tours, the [Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_Cup "Davis Cup"), and the [Billie Jean King Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Jean_King_Cup "Billie Jean King Cup").
