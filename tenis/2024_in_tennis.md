This page covers all the important events in the sport of **[tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis "Tennis")** in [2024](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024 "2024"). It provides the results of notable tournaments throughout the year on both the [ATP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_Tennis_Professionals "Association of Tennis Professionals") and [WTA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women%27s_Tennis_Association "Women's Tennis Association") Tours, the [Davis Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis_Cup "Davis Cup"), and the [Billie Jean King Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billie_Jean_King_Cup "Billie Jean King Cup"). Also notable is [Tennis at the 2024 Summer Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis_at_the_2024_Summer_Olympics "Tennis at the 2024 Summer Olympics"). It was the first time since 2002, where "[The Big 3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Three_%28tennis%29 "Big Three (tennis)")" ([Novak Djokovic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novak_Djokovic "Novak Djokovic"), [Roger Federer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Federer "Roger Federer") and [Rafael Nadal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Nadal "Rafael Nadal")) did not win either the [US Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Open_%28tennis%29 "US Open (tennis)"), [Wimbledon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wimbledon_Championships "Wimbledon Championships"), [Australian Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Open "Australian Open"), or [French Open](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Open "French Open").
