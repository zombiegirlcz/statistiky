## World Championships

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=2025_in_ice_hockey&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: World Championships")]

2025 World Ice Hockey Divisions for the [International Ice Hockey Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Ice_Hockey_Federation "International Ice Hockey Federation") (IIHF) will take place between December 9, 2024 and May 25, 2025.
