## World Championships

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=2024_in_ice_hockey&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: World Championships")]

2024 World Ice Hockey Divisions for the [International Ice Hockey Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Ice_Hockey_Federation "International Ice Hockey Federation") (IIHF) took place between December 10, 2023 and May 26, 2024.

### World Junior Ice Hockey Championships

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=2024_in_ice_hockey&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: World Junior Ice Hockey Championships")]

* December 26, 2023 – January 5: [2024 IIHF World Junior Championship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_IIHF_World_Junior_Championship "2024 IIHF World Junior Championship") in [![Sweden](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/4/4c/Flag_of_Sweden.svg/40px-Flag_of_Sweden.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden "Sweden") [Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gothenburg "Gothenburg")
  + The ![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/a/a4/Flag_of_the_United_States.svg/40px-Flag_of_the_United_States.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail) [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_men%27s_national_junior_ice_hockey_team "United States men's national junior ice hockey team") defeated ![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/4/4c/Flag_of_Sweden.svg/40px-Flag_of_Sweden.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail) [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden_men%27s_national_junior_ice_hockey_team "Sweden men's national junior ice hockey team"), 6–2, to win their sixth World Junior Ice Hockey Championship title.
  + ![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/c/cb/Flag_of_the_Czech_Republic.svg/40px-Flag_of_the_Czech_Republic.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail) [Czechia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic_men%27s_national_junior_ice_hockey_team "Czech Republic men's national junior ice hockey team") defeated ![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/bc/Flag_of_Finland.svg/40px-Flag_of_Finland.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail) [Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland_men%27s_national_junior_ice_hockey_team "Finland men's national junior ice hockey team"), 8–5, to win the bronze medal.
