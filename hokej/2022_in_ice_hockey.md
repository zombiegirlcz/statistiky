From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The following were the events of [ice hockey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_hockey "Ice hockey") for the year 2022 throughout the world.

## Winter Olympics and Paralympics

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=2022_in_ice_hockey&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Winter Olympics and Paralympics")]

The [2022 Winter Olympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Winter_Olympics "2022 Winter Olympics") and [Paralympics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Winter_Paralympics "2022 Winter Paralympics") took place in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing "Beijing"), China.

* February 9 – 20: [Ice hockey at the 2022 Winter Olympics – Men's tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_hockey_at_the_2022_Winter_Olympics_%E2%80%93_Men%27s_tournament "Ice hockey at the 2022 Winter Olympics – Men's tournament")
  + ![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/bc/Flag_of_Finland.svg/40px-Flag_of_Finland.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail) [Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland_men%27s_national_ice_hockey_team "Finland men's national ice hockey team") defeats ![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/02/Russian_Olympic_Committee_flag.png/40px-Russian_Olympic_Committee_flag.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail) [ROC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia_men%27s_national_ice_hockey_team "Russia men's national ice hockey team") 2–1 to win their first Olympic gold medal.
  + ![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/e/e6/Flag_of_Slovakia.svg/40px-Flag_of_Slovakia.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail) [Slovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovakia_men%27s_national_ice_hockey_team "Slovakia men's national ice hockey team") defeats ![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/4/4c/Flag_of_Sweden.svg/40px-Flag_of_Sweden.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail) [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden_men%27s_national_ice_hockey_team "Sweden men's national ice hockey team") 4–0 to win the bronze medal.

  + ![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d9/Flag_of_Canada_%28Pantone%29.svg/40px-Flag_of_Canada_%28Pantone%29.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail) [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_men%27s_national_ice_hockey_team "Canada men's national ice hockey team") defeats ![](//thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/thumb/a/a4/Flag_of_the_United_States.svg/40px-Flag_of_the_United_States.svg.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail) [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_men%27s_national_ice_hockey_team "United States men's national ice hockey team") 3–2 to win their fifth Olympic gold medal.

## IIHF World Championships

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=2022_in_ice_hockey&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: IIHF World Championships")]

2022 World Ice Hockey Divisions for the [International Ice Hockey Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Ice_Hockey_Federation "International Ice Hockey Federation") (IIHF) took place between December 12, 2021 and September 17, 2022.

[Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia "Russia") and [Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus "Belarus") were indefinitely suspended from international competition by the IIHF on February 28, 2022, due to the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine "Russian invasion of Ukraine").[[1]](#cite_note-1)
