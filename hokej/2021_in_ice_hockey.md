From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

The following were the events of [ice hockey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_hockey "Ice hockey") for the year 2021 throughout the world. In 2021, some hockey events were shortened, postponed, or canceled [due to the COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_of_the_COVID-19_pandemic_on_ice_hockey "Impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on ice hockey").

## [National Hockey League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Hockey_League "National Hockey League") (NHL)

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=2021_in_ice_hockey&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: National Hockey League (NHL)")]

* January 13 – May 16: [2020–21 NHL season](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_NHL_season "2020–21 NHL season")
  + The season was shortened and divisions were realigned due to the COVID-19 pandemic. The [All-Star Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_NHL_All-Star_Game "2023 NHL All-Star Game") (hosted by the [Florida Panthers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Panthers "Florida Panthers")) and the [Stadium Series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_NHL_Stadium_Series "2022 NHL Stadium Series") (hosted by the [Carolina Hurricanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carolina_Hurricanes "Carolina Hurricanes")) were postponed to [2023](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022%E2%80%9323_NHL_season "2022–23 NHL season"), while the [Winter Classic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_NHL_Winter_Classic "2022 NHL Winter Classic") (hosted by the [Minnesota Wild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota_Wild "Minnesota Wild")) was postponed to [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021%E2%80%932022_NHL_season "2021–2022 NHL season").
  + [Presidents' Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidents%27_Trophy "Presidents' Trophy") and West Division regular-season winners: [Colorado Avalanche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Colorado_Avalanche_season "2020–21 Colorado Avalanche season")
  + Central Division winners: [Carolina Hurricanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Carolina_Hurricanes_season "2020–21 Carolina Hurricanes season")
  + East Division winners: [Pittsburgh Penguins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Pittsburgh_Penguins_season "2020–21 Pittsburgh Penguins season")
  + North Division winners: [Toronto Maple Leafs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Toronto_Maple_Leafs_season "2020–21 Toronto Maple Leafs season")
  + [Art Ross Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Art_Ross_Trophy "Art Ross Trophy") winner: [Connor McDavid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connor_McDavid "Connor McDavid") ([Edmonton Oilers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Edmonton_Oilers_season "2020–21 Edmonton Oilers season"))
* February 20 and 21: [NHL Outdoors at Lake Tahoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHL_Outdoors_at_Lake_Tahoe "NHL Outdoors at Lake Tahoe") at the Edgewood Tahoe Resort in [Lake Tahoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Tahoe "Lake Tahoe")
  + February 20: The [Colorado Avalanche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Colorado_Avalanche_season "2020–21 Colorado Avalanche season") defeated the [Vegas Golden Knights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Vegas_Golden_Knights_season "2020–21 Vegas Golden Knights season") 3–2
  + February 21: The [Boston Bruins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Boston_Bruins_season "2020–21 Boston Bruins season") defeated the [Philadelphia Flyers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Philadelphia_Flyers_season "2020–21 Philadelphia Flyers season") 7–3
* May 15 – July 7: [2021 Stanley Cup playoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Stanley_Cup_playoffs "2021 Stanley Cup playoffs")
  + July 7: The [Tampa Bay Lightning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Tampa_Bay_Lightning_season "2020–21 Tampa Bay Lightning season") defeated the [Montreal Canadiens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Montreal_Canadiens_season "2020–21 Montreal Canadiens season") four games to one in the [Stanley Cup Final](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Stanley_Cup_Final "2021 Stanley Cup Final") to win their second consecutive and third overall [Stanley Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Cup "Stanley Cup").
* July 23 – 24: [2021 NHL entry draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_NHL_entry_draft "2021 NHL entry draft") held via video conference call in [Secaucus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secaucus%2C_New_Jersey "Secaucus, New Jersey")
  + [#1:](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_first_overall_NHL_draft_picks "List of first overall NHL draft picks") [Owen Power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owen_Power "Owen Power") (to the [Buffalo Sabres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020%E2%80%9321_Buffalo_Sabres_season "2020–21 Buffalo Sabres season") from the [Michigan Wolverines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_Wolverines_men%27s_ice_hockey "Michigan Wolverines men's ice hockey"))
